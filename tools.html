BSR Desktop Application

Developed a Python-based desktop application for black-start restoration studies that integrates Julia/JuMP optimization models, PowerModelsONM.jl and PowerModelsDistribution.jl workflows, OpenDSS validation, Excel-based data handling, operating-day and contingency-scenario selection, automated visualization, restoration timeline plots, DER dispatch plots, storage state-of-charge profiles, and post-restoration network visualization.

GridResilience Studio

Founded and developed a research software platform for distribution-system analysis, optimization, restoration, validation, visualization, and diagnostics. The platform supports power flow, OPF, maximal load delivery, black-start restoration, scenario generation, OpenDSS validation, JSON/CSV/Excel reporting, and GridMate diagnostic assistance.
