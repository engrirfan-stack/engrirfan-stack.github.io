Research

My research centers on uncertainty-aware and resilient grid operation, with emphasis on optimization-driven restoration, physics-based simulation, DER and BESS coordination, converter-based resources, and resilient operation of critical power infrastructure.

Black-Start Restoration and Maximal Load Delivery

I develop mixed-integer and robust optimization methods for distribution-system restoration, including full-horizon black-start restoration, maximal load delivery, switching and radiality decisions, grid-forming island requirements, voltage and thermal limits, source capability, and storage energy-reserve constraints.

Uncertainty-Aware and Rolling-Horizon Restoration

My work studies load uncertainty, scenario-indexed validation, rolling-horizon prediction and control windows, recursive backtracking, and storage-capacity feasibility under changing operating conditions. These methods are designed to analyze how forecast errors and early restoration decisions affect downstream feasibility.

Automation Framework and BSR Desktop Application

I develop Python automation workflows and a desktop application for black-start restoration studies. The workflow extracts and preprocesses OpenDSS-based distribution-system models, selects operating days and contingency cases, runs Julia/JuMP optimization using PowerModelsONM.jl and PowerModelsDistribution.jl, validates results in OpenDSS, updates post-restoration system states, and generates Excel summaries, DER dispatch plots, storage state-of-charge profiles, and PowerPlots network visualizations.

GridResilience Studio

GridResilience Studio is my research software platform for distribution-system analysis, optimization, restoration, scenario generation, validation, visualization, and diagnostics. It integrates OpenDSS with Python, Julia/JuMP, PowerModelsONM.jl, PowerModelsDistribution.jl, Gurobi, Ipopt, PyQt, and post-processing/reporting tools.

Power Electronics, SST, and Power Quality

My converter-based power systems work includes MATLAB/Simulink modeling of solid-state transformer and UPQC architectures, renewable integration, multi-port converter operation, grid-forming and grid-following converter concepts, DC-link behavior, voltage sag/swell mitigation, harmonic reduction, and fault-condition performance.

Data-Center Power and Critical Infrastructure

I am extending restoration, optimization, forecasting, automation, and physics-based simulation methods toward data-center and critical power infrastructure. This direction includes SST-based data-center architecture, resilient power and energy routing, BESS coordination, battery health monitoring, bidirectional AC/DC power flow, and converter-interfaced high-density electric systems.
