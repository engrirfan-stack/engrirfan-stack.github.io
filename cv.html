Research Profile

Final-year Ph.D. researcher in Electrical Engineering specializing in computational power-system modeling, optimization, and AI-assisted decision methods for resilient electric power systems. Five years of experience developing optimization models, research software, and automated simulation workflows using Python, Julia/JuMP, Gurobi, PowerModelsONM.jl, PowerModelsDistribution.jl, and OpenDSS. Research spans mixed-integer and robust optimization, decomposition-based methods, rolling-horizon decision making, dynamic programming, DER/BESS coordination, uncertainty-aware simulation, and optimization-generated datasets for AI models.

Selected Research Projects and Contributions

  • Sequential Black-Start Restoration Using PowerModelsONM.jl: Full-horizon optimization coordinating DER dispatch, BESS operation, switching, radiality, voltage feasibility, and prioritized load restoration with automated feeder conversion, optimization, validation, and visualization workflows.
  • Two-Stage Robust Maximal Load Delivery: Decomposition-based robust optimization for load restoration under block-level demand uncertainty, evaluated on a modified Iowa 240-node feeder.
  • Rolling-Horizon and Uncertainty-Aware Restoration: Prediction-horizon analysis, early battery dispatch, forecast error, path-dependent switching, recursive backtracking, and sequential storage-capacity reinforcement.
  • AI-Assisted Distribution-System Restoration: Optimization-based restoration trajectories, uncertain load and renewable generation modeling, physical constraints, and feasibility evaluation of learned decisions in decision-transformer research.
  • Converter-Based Power Quality and Energy Integration: MATLAB/Simulink modeling of solid-state transformer and UPQC architectures, including renewable integration, multi-port converter operation, DC-link behavior, voltage sag/swell mitigation, harmonic reduction, and fault-condition performance.
  • Data-Center Power and Energy Routing: Multi-port solid-state-transformer architectures for resilient data-center power systems with renewable generation and BESS, bidirectional AC/DC power routing, coordinated source/storage operation, power-quality support, and resilient energy management.
  • BSR Desktop Application: Python-based desktop application integrating Julia optimization, OpenDSS validation, Excel-based data handling, operating-day and contingency selection, automated visualization, and result reporting for black-start restoration studies.
